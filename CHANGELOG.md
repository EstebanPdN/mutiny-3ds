# Changelog

All notable changes to Mutiny 3DS are documented here.

## [1.0] - 2026-11-06

First release.

### Added

- Native Nintendo 3DS port of *Mutiny* (Nitrome, 2008): the ActionScript 2 game logic rewritten
  in C, with a MovieClip runtime for the original Flash animations, masks and sounds.
- All 15 story levels, all 18 two-player battle maps and all 15 weapons.
- Two-screen interface: the game on the top screen; team health, a live minimap and the turn's
  controls on the touch screen.
- Stylus aiming (touch, pull back, let go), drag-to-preview for pirates and weapons, and minimap
  camera dragging.
- Nitrome intro on start-up, two-screen pause and result windows with match information, and
  local best scores.
- HOME Menu icon, banner and banner sound.
- Test match: START + SELECT starts a two-player battle where every pirate has 5 of every weapon.

### Changed from the original

- Two-player treasure chests hold 1 to 5 random weapon types, 1 to 10 of each, never including
  a weapon that is already infinite for the pirate who opens them.
- Online high scores and the nitrome.com features are not available.
